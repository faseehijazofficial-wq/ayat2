# Storefront assets

The hero carousel uses three compressed 1800 × 900 compositions made from the three supplied campaign photos. The first photo's top heading was cropped out to avoid duplicating the website's overlay copy; the originals are not modified. The 1200 × 630 social preview uses the matching dark-green-and-gold theme.

The supplied Ayat Hair Oil emblem is saved as `ayat-hair-oil-logo.png` and is used in the header, footer, favicon, and social preview. The logo file is a resized copy; the original attachment is not modified. The supplied photos are also kept unchanged; the hero backgrounds are derived copies.

`ayat-hair-oil-product-150ml.jpg` is a square product-card crop from the first hero composition, created so the confirmed 150 ml product appears separately in the homepage listing and on the Products page.
